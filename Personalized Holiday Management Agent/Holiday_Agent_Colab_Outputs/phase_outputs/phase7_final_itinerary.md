## Day 1: Arrival and Acclimatization

Arrive at Charles de Gaulle Airport (approx. 1-hour drive to city center). Check-in to your hotel and freshen up. Explore the charming neighborhood of Le Marais for dinner and atmosphere. Try a classic French bistro like Le Loir dans la Theière (budget, €20-€30 per meal).

* Best time to visit: Evening
* Practical tip: Book a hotel in a central location to minimize travel time.
* Approximate cost bracket: Budget

## Day 2: Montmartre and the Latin Quarter

Visit the Musée de Montmartre (approx. 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM) to learn about the area's artistic history. Explore the winding streets and artist studios of Montmartre, where you can find unique souvenirs and street performers. Wander through the charming streets of the Latin Quarter, stopping for coffee and croissants at a charming café like Les Deux Magots (budget, €10-€20 per person).

* Best time to visit: Morning
* Practical tip: Wear comfortable shoes for the steep streets of Montmartre.
* Approximate cost bracket: Budget
